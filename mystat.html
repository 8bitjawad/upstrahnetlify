UPSTRAH is the first-of-its-kind platform combining performance tracking, networking, and community support. Designed to connect athletes, coaches, and fans to keep your passion for sport alive and growing.

What UPSTRAH Brings You

Fitness Stats

Track your daily fitness progress, monitor stamina, and stay on course to achieve your goals.

Practice Stats

Get insights from your training sessions and identify ways to keep improving your game.

Game Stats

Record and revisit your match performances—your stats are safely stored to look back on anytime.

Post

Share your highlights, achievements, and stories with the community to inspire and grow together.

Chat

Connect directly with teammates, coaches, or friends through one-on-one chats.

News

Catch up with the latest happenings from across the global sports world.

Peer Review

Show support for fellow athletes by dropping feedback, encouragement, or a quick note.

Club Connect

Stay updated with your club’s announcements, events, and activities—all in one place.
