Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

A. INTRODUCTION.

1. This privacy policy governs your use of our application Upstrah and/or website https://www.upstrah.com/ ("Application"). During your use of our application, we may, with your consent, collect your first name, last name, location and email address. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect, what we do with it, and how you can manage and control the use of your information shared with us.

B. WHAT IS THE INFORMATION YOU ARE SUBMITTING TO US?

1. With your consent, we may collect, personally identifiable information about you, either when you register an account with our website or the application or if you have a query or wish to hear from us. At the moment, the personally identifiable information we are collecting comprises of the following:

a. First Name
b. Last Name
c. Demographic information (including but not limited to gender, date of birth, BMI, other information related to performance in the specific sport)
d. Email address
e. Location
f. Contact number

2. In the event you engage us to provide any products and services to you, you may further share personally identifiable information such as the following:

a. Bank account number
b. Credit card number
c. Net banking details
d. UPI
e. Other

3. Please be assured that you have the option not to submit any personally identifiable data to us and non submission of such data shall affect your overall experience using our Application.

4. However, where the non submission of your data makes it legally, technically and/or commercially unviable to provide services to you, we may refuse to provide services or some functionalities or features of the services to you.

5. You may also demand that we stop any attempts at processing your data, on legitimate grounds, unless such processing is permitted by law. This shall affect your overall experience using our Application.

1. The personally identifiable information submitted by you to us will be used by us or affiliates for the following purposes:

a. To provide information about our products and services.
b. To answer any specific queries raised by you.
c. To send you promotional and marketing emails.
d. To provide the services or products for which you have registered yourself in our Application.
e. To process the data submitted by you.

2. We assure you that any personally identifiable information provided by you shall not be shared by us with third parties, unless you have provided explicit consent for the same. The data about your usage of our Application, the pages that you visit on our Application, the number of times you visit our Application and the duration of your stay on each page of our Application, reveal your preferences. a. Enhance the visual appearance of our Application
b. To improve the quality of text, the font and the content in our Application
c. To improve the overall look and feel of our Application
d. To solve any bugs or defects in the Application
e. To improve security features
f. To contact you regarding any matter within our knowledge that is likely to affect your privacy rights, your intellectual property rights or the safety of your person or property.

The data about your usage of our Application will not contain any personally identifiable information. We reserve the right to share any information or data that does not identify you, with third parties.

1. The information and data furnished to us may either be stored by us in a cloud server operated by a cloud service provider who has security features compliant with the applicable legal requirements. 3. We shall employ industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect such data, and we shall not retain this data for longer than is required either for the transaction in question or for compliance with statutory obligations.
